The Problem Statement

Manual Selection

Club recruitment is often biased, inconsistent, and slow — relying on in-person interviews and unstandardized criteria.

Certificate Farming

First-year students sometimes receive recognition without meaningful contribution, diluting merit signals.

Data Fragmentation

Academic records (SGPA), participation, and club data live in — preventing objective talent matching.

Proposed Solution

SYNAPSE is a digital portal that links students, clubs, and faculty into a single ecosystem — making selection transparent, consistent, and data-driven.

Unified Ecosystem

Single source of truth for profiles, participation logs, and society data — accessible by students and faculty.

Merit-First Engine

Automated talent allocation using priority data structures and merit scores derived from SGPA & activity metrics.

Tiered Certificate Logic

Certificates and privileges are unlocked based on transparent performance thresholds and contribution history.
